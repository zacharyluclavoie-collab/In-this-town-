# What works: performance reviews

Every 10 new posts on @inthisworld.story, the nightly run compares them using Buffer metrics
(average watch time, views, shares, likes, comments; Buffer doesn't give follows per post),
lists the top 3 and bottom 3, notes what the top ones share (rule type, length, ending),
and the next stories lean that way.

Reviewed up to: 0 posts (the new 40-55 s format started 2026-11-12).

## Baseline before the new format
- "Number above your head shows how many people love you" (50 s, posted by hand): 1,101 views,
  avg watch 19.9 s, 46 likes, 1 share. Best so far.
- "100 words a day" (59 s): 160 views, avg watch 16.3 s, 15 likes.
